> **Status:** FROZEN (CENTENNIAL EDITION)
> **Authority:** Supreme Source of Operational Truth

## Preamble

This Operations Constitution defines the enduring, technology-independent operational principles, capability lifecycles, and governance rules for the Enterprise Architecture Operating System (EAOS).

Technologies, container platforms, cloud providers, and programming languages will evolve across decades. This Constitution establishes the immutable operational laws that remain constant regardless of implementation changes.

---

## The 10 Operational Immutable Rules

- **OPS-001 (Observable):** Every business capability shall expose measurable real-time health and telemetry.
- **OPS-002 (Recoverable):** Every service shall be recoverable with defined RPO and RTO bounds.
- **OPS-003 (Auditable):** Every operational action shall produce immutable evidence trails.
- **OPS-004 (Reversible):** Every production change shall maintain a verified rollback strategy.
- **OPS-005 (Evidence-Based):** Objective operational telemetry authorizes change, not opinion.
- **OPS-006 (Governed Automation):** All operational automation shall operate under Policy as Code.
- **OPS-007 (Constitutional Supremacy):** Operational automation shall never bypass architectural rules.
- **OPS-008 (Technology Neutral):** Universal capabilities (Data, Cache, Graph) outlive transient tools.
- **OPS-009 (Capability Longevity):** Operational capabilities shall be organized around enduring enterprise needs.
- **OPS-010 (Enterprise Memory):** Incident lessons and operational knowledge shall be retained permanently.

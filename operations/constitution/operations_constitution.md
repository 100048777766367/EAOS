# EAOS Operations Constitution v1.0

> **Status:** FROZEN (CENTENNIAL EDITION)
> **Scope:** Enterprise Operations Governance (100-Year Life Span)

## Immutable Operations Rules

* **OPS-001 (Observability):** Every capability shall expose measurable health and telemetry.
* **OPS-002 (Recoverability):** Every service shall provide automated recovery procedures.
* **OPS-003 (Auditability):** Every operational decision shall leave immutable evidence.
* **OPS-004 (Reversibility):** Every change shall support automated rollback.
* **OPS-005 (Evidence Generation):** Every execution generates verifiable evidence artifacts.
* **OPS-006 (Governed Automation):** Every operational automation shall remain governed by policy.
* **OPS-007 (Constitutional Supremacy):** No automation shall bypass constitutional rules.
* **OPS-008 (Technology Independence):** Technology shall never define operations.
* **OPS-009 (Capability Permanence):** Operational capabilities shall outlive implementation tools.
* **OPS-010 (Organizational Memory):** Operational knowledge shall never be lost.
